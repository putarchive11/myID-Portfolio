## The book

Kenangan Si Kecil ("Little One's Keepsake") is a board book, with thick cardboard pages that are safe and durable in a child's hands. It holds photos and notes on your little one's milestones: the day they were born, their name and its meaning, the first smile, the first steps, all the way to the first birthday. Memories worth keeping, to open again together when they are older.
