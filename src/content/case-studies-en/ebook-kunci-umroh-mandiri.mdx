## The product

Kunci Umroh Mandiri is a guide package for pilgrims who want to perform umrah on their own, without a travel agency, in a way that is permissible and follows the Prophetic guidance. It has two parts that complement each other: an ebook to study before departure, and a toolkit app to use from preparation all the way to the Holy Land.

The package costs IDR 125,000 for both the ebook and the app. Purchase and full details are at [umrohsendiri.biz.id](https://www.umrohsendiri.biz.id).

## Inside the ebook

A 13-chapter ebook that guides you step by step, written in plain language with practical tips (chapter titles translated from Indonesian):

1. Independent Umrah: A Call You Can Answer Yourself
2. Planning Your Trip & Timing
3. Documents: The Foundation of Your Peace of Mind
4. The Independent Umrah Visa
5. Affordable Flights
6. Hotels & Accommodation
7. Transport & Connectivity
8. Preparing the Heart & Etiquette
9. Ihram, Miqat & Intention
10. Tawaf, Sa'i & Tahallul
11. In the Holy Land: Your Days There
12. Returning Home & Beyond
13. The Independent Umrah Survival Kit

## The toolkit app

A web app that can be installed on your phone's home screen and still opens without a signal. Buyers activate it with a personal activation code.

- **Preparation:** document and packing checklists, a preparation timeline, and a trip itinerary
- **Finances:** an umrah cost calculator, a savings calculator, and riyal-to-rupiah conversion
- **Worship:** the order of the pillars of umrah, miqat and the intention for ihram, tawaf, sa'i, and tahallul, complete with fully vowelled supplications
