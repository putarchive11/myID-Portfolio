## The project

Hanifah Edu is a planned free class for learning Arabic from scratch with the Durusul Lughah book, Volume 1. This page is still a **prototype**: the questions and teacher names are placeholders, and the site is not live yet. Its purpose is to show the learning flow before the real class opens.

## Learning flow

Students sign up and log in with their own account. Each day there is one video lesson of about 10 minutes, followed by 2 multiple-choice questions that immediately show the correct answer and an explanation. The next day only unlocks once today's questions are answered. At the end of the week there is a 7-question quiz, and the score is saved to the account.

## What I built

- A home page with a blackboard whose Arabic sentences change on their own, a weekly schedule, the syllabus, and a sign-up form
- A study room: a list of days that unlock step by step, a video player, lesson summaries, and daily questions
- Fully vowelled Arabic text in Uthmani script, with Latin transliteration and meaning
- An opening audio, plus the choice to learn by video or by audio with text
