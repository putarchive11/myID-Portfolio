## The event

Al-Hijaz Herbal took part in the 21st Islamic Book Fair from 20 to 24 September 2023 at Istora Gelora Bung Karno, Senayan, Jakarta, at stand A-11 on the second floor. I was part of the company committee that prepared the booth.

## My role

- Created the event poster marketing campaign
- Designed the booth banner, product flyers, and promotional price cards
- Led the committee's weekly meetings

## What I designed

The campaign told visitors where the booth was and which products they could buy: a tall banner featuring Al-Hijaz Herbal's best sellers, a "Coming Soon, Come Join Us" poster with the dates and stand number, flyers explaining each product, and price cards for the booth.
