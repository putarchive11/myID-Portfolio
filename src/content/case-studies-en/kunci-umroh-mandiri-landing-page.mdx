## The project

Kunci Umroh Mandiri is a guide for pilgrims who want to perform umrah without a travel agency. The product is a 13-chapter ebook plus a web app with preparation tools. I designed and built this landing page myself to explain what the guide covers and to sell the package. The site is live at [umrohsendiri.biz.id](https://www.umrohsendiri.biz.id).

## What I did

- Designed the visual identity: a Ka'bah logo, emerald green and gold colours, and the Source Serif 4 and Plus Jakarta Sans typefaces
- Built the page with Astro and Tailwind CSS, then deployed it on Vercel with automatic deploys from GitHub
- Connected a custom domain through the DNS settings
- Set the Arabic text in fully vowelled Uthmani script so verses and hadith display neatly
- Set up ordering via WhatsApp and questions via email

## Prayer times on the page

At the top there are two clock bars. The first shows Indonesian time and the next prayer, with the city following the visitor's phone time zone (WIB, WITA, or WIT). The second shows Makkah time. Everything is calculated right in the browser with no server, using the Ministry of Religious Affairs (Kemenag) method for Indonesia and Umm al-Qura for Makkah.

## What's on the page

The journey in 6 steps, the list of 13 ebook chapters, an ebook preview you can flip like a book, an app demo with 5 clickable tabs, pricing packages, an FAQ, and an always-visible WhatsApp chat button. The layout was tested on phones, including Safari on iPhone.
