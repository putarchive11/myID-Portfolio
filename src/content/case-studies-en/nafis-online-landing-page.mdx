## The project

Nafis Online sells honey, herbal drinks, herbal capsules, and Islamic books on online marketplaces. This landing page lets customers buy directly at lower prices than on the marketplace, then pay when the goods arrive. The site is live at [nafisonline.my.id](https://www.nafisonline.my.id).

## How it works

Customers choose products, add them to the cart, and fill in their name and address. The order, complete with item details and the total, opens right away in the admin's WhatsApp, ready to send. The site uses no database, so it stays light and easy to maintain.

## What I built

- The visual design: elegant serif headings, cream and navy colours, and product photos on a consistent background
- A product list loaded from a single CSV file, so the admin only needs to edit one table to add or change products
- Search, category filters, and a best-seller carousel
- Promotions calculated automatically in the cart: free shipping within Greater Jakarta and capped discounts
- An always-visible WhatsApp chat button and a connected custom domain
