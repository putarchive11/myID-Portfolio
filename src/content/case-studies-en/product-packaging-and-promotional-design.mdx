## Creative designer

As a creative designer, I created event posters for marketing and designed 7 new products registered with BPOM (Indonesia's food and drug authority). The packaging had to look attractive on the shelf while still carrying everything a registered product needs: the product name, description, net weight, registration number, and production details.

## Premium Black Garlic

A dark, premium-feel pouch for black garlic (*Allium Sativum*), with gold accents and a product photo so buyers can see what is inside.

## Digital marketing

Design goes hand in hand with sales. In digital marketing I sold more than 1,000 new products in a single month with average revenue of IDR 80 million per month, while building good relationships with sellers and providing after-sales service.
