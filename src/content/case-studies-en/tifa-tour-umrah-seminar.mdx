## The event

Tifa Tour, an umrah and hajj travel organizer, held a committee training called *Building the Character of an Umrah Entrepreneur* on Sunday, 7 August 2022 at Cafe Archeries. I served on the committee and received a committee certificate from the CEO of PT Tifa Jaya Abadi.

## My role

- Created the event poster
- Designed the umrah package schedule posters (Start Jakarta and Start CGK 2022 - 2023)
- Coordinated the seminar participants

## Posters

The umrah package posters list every departure date with quad, triple, and double room prices, the airline, and the route, so prospective pilgrims can compare packages at a glance. The same visual style was used across the seminar materials and certificates.
