## The event

Al-Hijaz Herbal joined the 21st Islamic Book Fair, held on 20 - 24 September 2023 at Istora Gelora Bung Karno, Senayan, Jakarta, at stand A-11 on the second floor. I was part of the office committee that prepared the booth.

## My role

- Created the event poster marketing campaign
- Designed the booth banner, product flyers and promotional price cards
- Led the committee's weekly meetings

## What I designed

The campaign told visitors where to find the booth and what to buy: a tall banner with the best-selling Al-Hijaz Herbal products, a "Segera Hadir & Ramaikan" poster with the date and stand number, flyers explaining each product, and price cards for the booth.
