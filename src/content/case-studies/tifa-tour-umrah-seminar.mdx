## The event

Tifa Tour, an umrah and hajj travel provider, held the training "Committee Training for Developing Umrah Entrepreneur Character" (seminar *Membentuk Karakter Pengusaha Umrah*) on Sunday, 7 August 2022 at Cafe Archeries. I served on the committee (panitia) and received a committee certificate from the CEO of PT Tifa Jaya Abadi.

## My role

- Created the event posters
- Designed the umrah package schedule posters (Start Jakarta and Start CGK 2022 - 2023)
- Coordinated the seminar participants

## Posters

The umrah package posters list every departure date with quad, triple and double room prices, the airline and the route, so customers can compare packages at a glance. The same visual style was carried into the seminar materials and the certificate.
